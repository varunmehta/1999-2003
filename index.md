# 1999-2003 Web Development Archive

This is an archive of all my web development antics and experiments with digital art from 1999-2003. A time when I was more into HTML and was learning the ropes of ASP and Java. Even though I have lost most of the source code, I was able to get the final files.

It was an interesting journey trying to retrieve the information from my old tripod.com account. I was able to get a lot of my old stuff back! Some of the digital art images seem to have been corrupted, with no way to retrieve them.

Somewhere around 2000-2001, if you searched "varun mehta" or "varun info" I would be in the top 3 results on Google.

## Technical Notes

All Flash content on these pages has been restored using [Ruffle](https://ruffle.rs/), an open-source Flash Player emulator written in Rust. This allows the Flash animations and interactive content to work in modern browsers without the need for the deprecated Adobe Flash Player plugin.

The sites are preserved as close to their original state as possible, maintaining the design aesthetic and functionality of the early 2000s web.
